## Final interface

Entry point remains main(api), returning JSON-compatible completion_proposed and evidence. This is a repair of the existing executable method, not a motion command or an episode reset.

Gate dependencies and recovery:
1. Source model: retain the measured supported package rectangle, thickness, semantic print fiducials, and support plane only during the noncontact approach while a current top sample and visual identity/pose comparison remain consistent. Changed or ambiguous source identity stops the replan. Initial survey and failed-depth replacement logic are unchanged.
2. Replanned descent: needs the open-hand geometry, source side intervals, and the nearby bowl's external envelope, not a fresh full package transform. A measurement-only review supplies six spread rim-surface depth samples and six to eight visible outer-boundary pixels in an explicitly identified camera. geo.plane fits the measured rim surface; api.plane_point intersects only identified visible boundary rays; geo.circle_xy fits the exterior boundary. A reviewed upright, flared-wall model bounds the visible bowl wall by that exterior cylinder. Fit residuals enlarge its heuristic uncertainty. If the needed view is unavailable, one separately justified 20 mm upward observation action is allowed, followed by one fresh attempt; otherwise the exact evidence gap is reported.
3. Eccentric grasp feasibility: compute a sliding direction from the measured long package edge and verify compatibility with the current gripper opening axis. Intersect the finite positive-overlap intervals for both pads, including pad skew, measurement uncertainty, and controller tolerance. Search at most 42 candidate offsets, preferring the smallest displacement. The full supplied finger-envelope XY box, rather than just the pads, must clear the measured exterior bowl bound with uncertainty and rotation allowance. This bound applies only to the proposed vertical descent. The high relocation and descent each retain their separate local-action review, including other external objects. A directly observed obstruction still forbids motion.
4. Actual alignment and closure: derive the vertical pad target and stopping tolerance from the measured side-overlap/support-clearance interval. After descent, recompute finite long-side overlap, vertical overlap, opening slack, table clearance, and bowl clearance from actual proprioception and own geometry. These are geometric witnesses, not contact evidence. A fresh visual flanking/support review and an obtainable current item motion reference are still required before closure. Entire pad-band containment and complete perimeter visibility are not required. A failed actual geometric check stops with its numerical values instead of repeatedly commanding an unchanged target.
5. Retention: unchanged independent RGB-D patch or semantic-fiducial observations are compared with measured whole-action robot motion using geo.attachment_error. A new identified patch may establish a local test reference without old-shape registration. Unknown tracking triggers the existing correspondence/new-patch recovery, not assumed attachment. Trial count persists and remains bounded at three.
6. Transport: full envelope registration is required only when transporting or positioning the package envelope. Existing quality-checked persistent tracker handles, semantic correspondence recovery, and the justified upward-view fallback remain. Current local retention alone is not fabricated into global pose. Transfer has at most eight 55 mm legs, with local obstruction reviews and measured attachment checks. Reached pose does not establish an object effect; stalled motion stops.
7. Bowl and release: the original measured inner opening remains a separate destination model from the new exterior obstacle model. Current separated inner-rim samples must corroborate it before the relevant raise, lower, and release actions. Actual transported radial containment, object/finger height, and bounded drop height gate opening. The shifted grasp does not shift the placement goal: transfer still centers the observed object envelope over the bowl.
8. Final effect: after acknowledged opening and a retreat, two fresh reviews measure actual interior support, gripper separation, identifiable package points, current inner rim, and stability across at least six native steps. Neither tracking nor a release command substitutes for this gate. Missing final evidence produces an incomplete result without regrasping.

All reviews declare exact structured fields, check measurement_errors and measurement membership, and use explicit unknown outcomes. Local action reviews distinguish external obstruction, self-occlusion, and unobserved space; none_observed is not certified clearance. The revised exterior model is a short-horizon observation-based approximation, not a certified collision model. It is invalid after relevant item/bowl interaction or inconsistent current observations.

Persistence remains six bounded checkpoint keys. The compact descent plan, fit provenance, overlap limits, and exterior bound are added to geom; no clouds or nested histories are saved. Existing tracker handles and semantic definitions are retained and freshly updated after repair. Jaw transitions and phase evidence continue to persist. The current approach phase is resumed from the actual high, open-hand state; no action is assumed undone. Existing review/control reserves, bounded leases, exception reporting, release-intent recovery, and completion-proposal limitations remain in force.

## Final Jev input/output cooperation

The seven existing Jev control questions are preserved. Each translation or rotation question explicitly identifies its world axis and uses its compiler-bound goal_relation: positive for increase, negative for decrease, hold for within_tolerance. The gripper uses exactly open, close, or keep from the objective. The host executes Jev's answers unchanged through api.control, including every api.drive update. Quaternions remain xyzw, rotation errors are world rotation vectors, and targets use tool_point=pad_center rather than confusing TCP with the pads.

Python computes the new outer-rim fit, uncertainty allowances, finite pad-side overlap interval, full finger-envelope bounds, candidate shifts, vertical feasibility interval, and stopping tolerance. Jev is not asked to calculate those quantities or infer contact. Physical-command features continue to identify phase and historical gating; the host supplies fresh robot state, residuals, and axis relations. Actual post-descent geometry is checked numerically before the unchanged visual alignment and retention gates.

The new measurement-only review declares item_prior and bowl_prior as consistent/changed/unknown, wall_model as upright_flared_within_outer_rim/unknown, the exact camera enum, a nullable typed outer_boundary pixel array, and view as upward_2cm_supported/none. Its question explicitly defines those meanings, requests rim_surface0 through rim_surface5 plus o, and distinguishes a rejected centered descent from the proposed offset replan. An upward-view choice only authorizes the separately described bounded observation action when current local evidence supports it. No review choice overrides an observed obstruction.

Existing local-action reviews still separate external, visibility, basis, and view outcomes. Existing correspondence, patch recovery, retention comparisons, jaw receipts, and final inside_supported/rim_or_outside/still_held/unknown interpretation remain unchanged. Completion is only a measured proposal after post-release relation and stability checks, never an official evaluator result.

## Original complete model design (historical if revised)

This method measures the package, its support, and the bowl from the current RGB-D observations. It uses a conservative upright-box model only after checking visible surface geometry and support. Grasping is gated by local finger/side geometry, not by continued visibility of every corner. Retention is tested by independently observed package motion versus measured robot motion. A persistent textured-patch reference normally supplies that observation; identifiable physical landmarks and a new local patch provide bounded recovery. Full package-envelope registration is requested only when transport and placement need it. Release is followed by a retreat, fresh bowl/item observations, and a separate stability comparison. All motion is bounded, mediated by Jev, and checkpointed; an unmet evidence gate produces an incomplete result rather than an assumed success.

## Model revision1 rationale (historical)

The acknowledged execution only completed the high approach. The rejected descent was genuinely obstructed by the bowl-side outer finger, not by missing visibility. This repair replaces the centered-descent assumption with a measured, bounded longitudinal grasp replan while the jaws remain open and high. It measures the bowl's exterior rim, accounts for the complete supplied finger envelope, and searches the package's finite opposing-side overlap interval for a shifted grasp that avoids that obstruction. The shifted high approach and descent still require separate current local-obstruction reviews; no rejected path is authorized. It also derives the descent tolerance from the available vertical contact/support interval and checks actual post-descent overlap, opening clearance, and finger clearance before closure. Existing patch tracking, independent retention tests, transport, release, final verification, checkpoints, and Jev definitions are preserved.
