## Original model design (historical)

This is an observation-driven pick-and-place method, not a stored trajectory. The user's sentence is supplied as task data to the first visual review. The method acquires a planar package model, a supported-height estimate, persistent printed-feature identities, and the receiving opening from current RGB-D. It explicitly limits its geometric model to a flat-sided package and an approximately horizontal round receptacle; unsupported interpretations produce an incomplete result rather than invented geometry. Contact and retention are separate: a guarded pinch is followed by a small diagnostic lift, and independently observed package motion is compared with actual robot motion. A textured patch is initialized before occlusion when possible. Unknown tracking invokes current semantic-landmark registration, with one explicitly justified view-changing attempt rather than assuming attachment. Transfer uses local observation gates, distinguishes visible external obstacles from self-occlusion and unobserved space, and preserves the grasp orientation. Release occurs just above the measured opening with the fingers above its rim. Fresh post-release images and a persistent visible feature assess containment and stability. Checkpoints preserve measurement provenance, landmark definitions, phase intent, and attachment evidence across repairs. All loops and retries are bounded, and model/control resources are reserved for final verification.
