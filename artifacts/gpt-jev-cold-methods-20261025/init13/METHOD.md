The final source and final interface are authoritative. Model descriptions below are preserved verbatim. Earlier design/rationales are historical, not additional runtime instructions.

## Final interface

main(api) and the completion_proposed/evidence result are unchanged. Checkpoint reuse requires matching frame, TCP pose, orientation, and closed-jaw state. Tracker handles are newly initialized and never restored as live handles. The patch adds current calibrated bowl crops, an optional source-versus-bowl chromatic reference measured before transfer, and a review-free post-release assessment. The latter checks a current rim fit, registered source geometry or an adequately extended planar component matching the measured carton dimensions, location below the lip, separation from the withdrawn hand, and stability over additional acknowledged steps. Component identity remains a measured appearance-and-shape hypothesis, not segmentation truth. Missing geometry, ambiguous appearance, inadequate spatial coverage, exhausted observation resources, or failed retention returns incomplete. Existing typed review fields and landmark definitions are preserved. All new API calls use documented arguments and return keys.

## Final Jev input/output cooperation

The existing seven explicitly bound axis and gripper questions are preserved unchanged. For each world translation or rotation axis, increase maps to positive, decrease to negative, and within_tolerance to hold. The gripper selects the requested open, close, or keep objective unchanged. Python computes targets, residuals, envelope heights, uncertainty intervals, appearance scores, and geometric comparisons. Jev receives the existing phase, authorized-local-action, uncertainty, and target features; it is not asked to calculate geometry or interpret images. Above-rim motion uses the saved narrowly scoped observational authorization only after unchanged-frame revalidation and the previously measured retention lift. Negative robust clearance is reported as uncertainty, not certified fit or a detected obstacle. A single release trial is permitted only from a newly measured centered pose above the lip; no pushing through a rim or repeated stalled contact is added. Post-release retreat follows the traversed neighborhood at increased height, and settling uses keep at the measured current pose.

## Original complete model design (historical if revised)

This method interprets the supplied instruction as runtime goal data and measures the source carton, its supporting surface, and the bowl from current RGB-D. It generates a short-side grasp and a centered, low-height release from those measurements, not from stored poses. It uses a textured patch when available, independently compares observed object motion with measured hand motion, and recovers unknown registration using persistently defined printed landmarks. Local motion reviews distinguish external obstruction, self-occlusion, and unobserved space; they authorize bounded progress or a specific view change, not certified collision-free paths. After release, a new view and two separated observations assess containment, support, and stability. Geometry, motion evidence, and phase checkpoints support repair without reopening an already held item. Any unsupported geometry, ambiguous registration, failed retention test, stalled motion, or exhausted reserve produces an incomplete result with the relevant measurements.

## Model revision1 rationale (historical)

The previous method repeatedly raised the wrist without changing the bowl-facing finger's lateral relationship to the rim. The recorded review also indicates that the bowl moved, so its initial obstacle geometry must not be reused. This repair reacquires source correspondence and current inner/outer rim measurements in one review, then searches bounded long-axis grasp offsets and small yaw changes using the measured finger envelope. It permits an approach only when the resulting local separation and carton contact support have numerical margins, followed by a fresh visual straddling check. A single explicitly supported upward observation move precedes the changed approach; repeated upward views are not the recovery. Review calls are consolidated, and already traversed vertical space and measured above-rim geometry support subsequent bounded motions without redundant reviews. Retention tracking, landmark recovery, release, and two post-release observations remain required. Opening-fit margins now combine the reported observation uncertainty, bowl-fit error, and controller allowance rather than treating a heuristic uncertainty as an undocumented three-sigma certificate.

## Model revision2 rationale (historical)

The failed opening fit mixed lip samples with a point about 25 mm down the bowl interior. This repair separates the evidence needed for approach from that needed for placement. Approach uses current, robustly fitted outer-lip measurements and source landmark registration; it no longer requires an inner-opening reconstruction before a grasp can start. At placement, a measured outer-lip plane supports calibrated ray intersections at explicitly identified visible inner-lip pixels, avoiding mixed boundary depth without inventing hidden contours. The outer and inner circles remain separate models with recorded residuals, angular support, and uncertainty. Current partial measurements are checkpointed before fitting. A locally supported above-source adjustment can reuse that review, and longer bounded feedback leases avoid spending another review merely because a lease was too short. Retention, unknown-registration recovery, current destination measurements, actual release, and two post-release assessments are preserved.

## Model revision3 rationale (historical)

The failure conflated recovery of the complete original landmark set with the evidence needed for a retention-measurement lift. The visible printed face and identifiable short end can provide a new measured registration without exposing the occluded perimeter. This repair fits that current face, intersects rays at two explicitly identified physical end corners, checks the measured end length and an independent saved printed landmark, and initializes a new textured patch before lifting. It never obtains registration from commanded hand motion. The existing measured box dimensions and semantic references remain historical invariants, with propagated registration uncertainty. The repair also addresses the directly coupled observation budget: one recovery review can measure the adjacent bowl and distinguish local transfer support, external obstruction, and self-occlusion. Missing placement evidence does not prevent a justified retention lift. A supported transfer uses the measured rim and envelope checks rather than repeating reviews at every leg. Final placement gets a fresh review and a measured post-release stability interval, with a second visual comparison available if tracking fails. No grasp or completion is claimed from closure, tracking alone, or intended placement.

## Model revision4 rationale (historical)

The failed check treated overlapping uncertainty allowances as proof that the carton could not fit, and applied a release-fit requirement before an independently justified above-rim observation move. The nominal measured radial clearance was 6.16 mm; the summed allowances were 7.87 mm. This repair preserves both numbers and distinguishes nominal incompatibility from an uncertified but supported placement trial. It does not reduce the saved measurement uncertainty. Above-rim motion remains subject to measured envelope checks; release requires newly observed centering, positive clearance after the source-dimension and aperture allowances, and fingers above the lip. Only one release trial is authorized. The repair also addresses the actual remaining resources: there are no GPT observations left. An unchanged-frame checkpoint is reused without pretending it is a new measurement, and its identified patch is reinitialized for subsequent independent RGB-D motion observations. Final verification can use either that registration or a freshly measured, geometrically checked source-color/face component, with current rim measurements and a separate settling interval. Unknown registration still has the existing semantic-landmark review recovery when a review is available; otherwise it stops with the exact evidence gap. No physical reset or reopening during transfer is introduced.
