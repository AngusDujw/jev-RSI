## Original model design (historical)

This is an observation-driven carton-to-bowl method, not a replay of image coordinates or a stored trajectory. It measures the carton's top and supporting surface, its visible footprint, and the bowl's inner opening and interior. It aligns the measured gripper opening axis with the short carton dimension, checks the actual preclosure arrangement, and uses a small measured lift to test retention. A textured RGB-D patch is the normal motion measurement; semantically defined, independently remeasured landmarks provide an executable fallback, including after a repair. Transfer uses bounded local legs, distinguishes external obstruction from incomplete visibility and self-occlusion, and checks retained motion against actual robot motion. Release occurs above the measured opening with both the carton and finger envelope accounted for. Separate post-release observations assess containment, separation, and stability. Checkpoints preserve reference definitions, measured invariants, preclosure evidence, and acknowledged phases. Geometry, registration, local-motion, resource, and final-observation failures return an incomplete result without claiming success.

The repair checks overlap of that band with the measured carton side, jointly accounting for support clearance and positional uncertainty, and checks the estimated inner opening rather than the outer pad envelope. This is inferred geometry with reported heuristic uncertainty, not an assumed attachment or a fabricated registration.
