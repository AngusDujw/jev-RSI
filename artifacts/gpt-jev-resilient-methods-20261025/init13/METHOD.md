## Final interface

main(api) and the existing checkpoints and phases are preserved. All new records are bounded independent checkpoints.

1. Repair binding: requires the same physical episode at frame 273 unless a continuation checkpoint already exists. The supplied review establishes surface identity, persistent q-mark definitions, the near-end direction, locally justified above-rim progress, and no observed external obstruction. Current cloud, point, and calibrated plane-ray measurements must corroborate that evidence. The previously failed q0 depth is replaced by a ray intersection at its visible junction, not by an assumed object transform. Its uncertainty is retained. Current corresponding marks independently recheck the saved lift against measured TCP motion.
2. Envelope: requires the current end plane and visible width direction, measured width consistency, and the saved rigid dimensions. The world-upper seam remains unknown. All measured interior points instead bound the end's vertical translation; the envelope includes the full resulting height interval. The hidden underside is not presented as a measured surface. The saved inward-direction hypothesis is valid only while current end orientation, identity, and rigid-motion checks agree.
3. Motion: a small identified RGB-D template is checkpointed with its camera, pixels, world measurements, appearance, and frame. Current color correspondence and independently fitted rigid geometry determine its transform. Competing correspondences contribute explicit uncertainty or cause rejection. Robot motion is not used to generate its measured transform. The existing qualified RGB-D tracker is a separate alternative; neither registration method alone proves retention. Actual hand motion is compared with observed object motion after every bounded leg. If both measurements fail and no review remains, the exact registration gap is reported.
4. Route and release: retain the current partially corroborated stationary bowl only for the previously reviewed short noncontact route. Preserve orientation, use actual finger offsets relative to pad_center, keep the object and fingers above the rim during horizontal motion, and require positive conservative containment before opening. External obstruction, changed geometry, inconsistent motion, or stalled control invalidates continuation. Self-occlusion is not treated as an obstacle or as certified clearance. Existing finite leases, progress checks, reserves, and acknowledged jaw checkpoints remain in force.
5. Final assessment without a model call: obtain a new agentview after withdrawal. Fresh rim measurements must agree with the stationary bowl hypothesis. A distinct near-horizontal surface must appear where the pre-transfer view showed bowl interior, match the package dimensions, and admit only contained, below-rim finite-envelope hypotheses at the sampled orientation resolution. Account for that resolution in the geometric margin. Measured finger separation and subsequent RGB-D stability support a placement proposal; support is inferred from the observed settled relation, not from a force sensor. A missing or ambiguous final witness stops assessment without disturbing the released package.

The repair adds at most 48 template samples and 96 pre-transfer bowl samples in separate checkpoint values. Historical frame identification is never reused as a fresh detection after motion. Every new API call uses the documented schema; the existing structured review calls remain available only when their budgets permit. Completion remains a proposal, not official success.

## Final Jev input/output cooperation

The existing seven Jev questions and exact choice keys are unchanged. Each translation or rotation question uses its explicit world-axis goal_relation: increase maps to positive, decrease to negative, and within_tolerance to hold. The gripper question follows open, close, or keep exactly. Python computes geometry, uncertainty, residual magnitudes, pad-center targets, and tolerances; Jev is not asked to calculate metrics or act as an image sensor.

The repaired continuation retains api.drive for bounded pose-feedback leases and api.control for acknowledged opening and stability intervals. Ordinary route legs remain at most 0.035 m, with control translation limited to 0.02 m. Features identify purpose, local-model scope, and current envelope uncertainty. Reached, moved, stalled, and lease_exhausted are controller outcomes only. A progressing exhausted lease is remeasured before further motion; a stalled or nonprogressing lease stops. Independent current RGB-D motion and actual robot state establish retention evidence. Release is followed by a new observation and a measured stability interval. No choice is silently corrected and no review call is attempted by the new zero-budget branch.

## Original complete model design (historical if revised)

This method binds the user's instruction as runtime observation context and derives the manipulation geometry from current RGB-D. It uses a measured rectangular-package model and a measured bowl opening, not stored object coordinates or a trajectory. The gates are factored: noncontact approach retains a checked short-horizon stationary prior; closure is only an acknowledged jaw transition; a small lift independently compares observed surface motion with actual hand motion; transfer uses a refreshed transported envelope; and release is followed by a new view, a settling interval, independent stability measurements, and a fresh goal-relation review. A new local patch can support a retention test without first recovering the old global shape pose. Unknown tracking has landmark and new-reference recovery paths. All motions are bounded, obstruction and visibility are separate review outcomes, and persistent checkpoints distinguish historical evidence from current measurements.
