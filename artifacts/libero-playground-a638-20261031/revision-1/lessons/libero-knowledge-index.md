## Status meanings
- hypothesis: authored or proposed, without a demonstrated effect in the supplied evidence.
- scene-specific: supported by this scene or one manipulation result; not established as a general success.
- verified: repeated supporting observations for the explicitly limited claim. This revision uses this status only for same-stack continuation across observed control slices, not whole-task reliability.

An acquisition effect was established once. Placement was not established. Neither repeated descriptions of the same frame nor a native outcome independently verifies a mechanism.
