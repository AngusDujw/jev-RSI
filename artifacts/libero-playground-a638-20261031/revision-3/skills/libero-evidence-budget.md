# Evidence feasibility

For both findings, **inputs/units** are actual review, synthesis, supervision, plan-revision, Jev, native-step, byte and key counters; documented costs; dependency graph of nominal/contingency evidence; current pending effects and physical scope. Counts are not interchangeable; frames are not elapsed time. **Responsibilities:** planner/program allocates and checks feasibility; reviewer answers scoped questions; Jev cannot replace observation or persistence capacity.

## A. Motion capacity is not evidence capacity

**Status:** verified narrowly for repeated resource mismatch in N and T; no reserve policy proved sufficient end to end.
**Applicability:** Independently limited manipulation resources.

Budget the entire remaining effect chain, including geometry, attachment refresh, release, withdrawal, settling, planning, generation, contract repair and outer supervision. Track plan revisions separately from GPT calls. Reserve supported persistence before requesting large outputs.

For each failed gate name the missing constraint and genuinely different resolving evidence/candidate. Distinguish missing measurements, numerical infeasibility, faulty gates, schema/storage failures and service errors. More motion does not repair every class. After unproductive revisions reconsider strategy rather than merely renaming stages.

N exhausted GPT capacity after acquisition with substantial motion capacity remaining. T terminated on plan_revision_budget with reported four GPT calls and 127 Jev calls/381 native steps remaining. This was not a final-placement test or zero-control-budget failure.
**Stop:** before an effect whose independent evidence cannot fit. Preserve state/command and seek supervision; do not lower margins or promote a prefix to completion.
**Evidence IDs:** N-SUM, N-PLAN, N-J1 through N-J10; T-SUM, T-PLAN, T-J5 through T-J8, T-RBUD. E-SUM retains the distinct service boundary.

## B. Concrete preflight and explicit reallocation

**Status:** scene-specific successful rejection at preflight; reallocation/integrated-stage sufficiency remains hypothesis.
**Applicability:** Necessary recovery costs conflict with protected downstream evidence.

T required two in-program reviews, one outer review and 16 protected placement calls: 19 total. It found 18 and spent no in-program review or motion. Later public count was 17: stationary synthesis/supervision still consumes resources. Subsequent explicit reallocation was defeated by geometry/schema failures. Final stage integration combined compatible questions but stopped on disturbance after its second review.

Enumerate mandatory evidence by action boundary, include costs of reaching preflight and bounded repair, and reconcile counters after synthesis/reviews/supervision. Combine only compatible same-frame questions; future effects cannot be authorized in advance. Reallocate explicitly while retaining mandatory evidence or stop, rather than repeatedly synthesizing under an impossible fixed reserve.
**Stop:** exact shortfall blocks dependent work. A smaller reserve is not proof all checks fit; resource rejection is not obstruction.
**Evidence IDs:** T-M05, T-J5, T-RBUD, T-PLAN, T-M06, T-M08, T-J6 through T-J8; N-M10's earlier reserve repair remained unexecuted.
