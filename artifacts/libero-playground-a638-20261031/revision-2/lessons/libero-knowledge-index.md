## Status meanings
- hypothesis: proposed or authored, without a demonstrated effect.
- scene-specific: supported by a local measurement, failure or manipulation result; general reliability is unestablished.
- verified: repeated supporting observations for the explicitly limited claim, not universal success or isolated causal superiority.

Two acquisition lifts have measured retention evidence. Episode 1 adds tracker corroboration to one of them; two channels on that lift are not two manipulation trials. Neither episode establishes completed placement.
