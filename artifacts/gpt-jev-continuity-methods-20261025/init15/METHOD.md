# Actual complete GPT method: initial state15

The final source and final interface are authoritative. Model descriptions below are preserved verbatim. Earlier design/rationales are historical, not additional runtime instructions.

## Final interface

The assembled method retains main(api), returning JSON-compatible completion_proposed and evidence. No new API operations or review fields are introduced. Existing episode checkpoints and tracker handles are reused with fresh observations; historical geometry is not treated as a current detection. Internal lease and move helpers gain an optional position_tolerance, defaulting to the existing 0.003 m. move also gains assess_effect, which returns after one reviewed local lease or view action so its caller can measure the object effect. Release alignment uses a tolerance clamped to the documented 0.0005..0.003 m range, derived from measured centered footprint radius, current registration uncertainty, and the existing conservative opening radius. It performs at most three corrections, rechecks retention and placement after motion, and preserves an already acceptable release height. Stalls remain explicit stops. A successful geometric gate only authorizes release; acknowledged opening, retreat, actual interior placement, and measured post-release stability are still separate requirements.

## Final Jev input/output cooperation

Existing Jev questions and dispatch are preserved. Each world translation and rotation question explicitly selects its own processed_features.axes.<axis>.goal_relation: increase means positive, decrease means negative, and within_tolerance means hold. The gripper question follows objective.gripper using exactly open, close, or keep. api.drive still invokes real api.control decisions, with unchanged Jev choices and numerically bounded magnitudes. Alignment requests keep the jaws unchanged and use pad_center targets, the saved xyzw orientation, and the newly computed local tolerance. Python computes footprint radii, uncertainty margins, targets, and acceptance criteria; Jev is not asked to calculate them or infer hidden contact. Existing structured image reviews distinguish an observed external obstacle, no observed external obstacle, self-occlusion, and unobserved space before a local action. A reached pose, a track, or jaw delivery alone never establishes placement or task completion.

## Original complete model design (historical if revised)

The method acquires the package, support, and bowl geometry from current calibrated RGB-D rather than using the pictured pixel coordinates as a trajectory. It uses a measured rectangular top face, a visible support surface, and the bowl's inner rim to derive an orientation-aware side grasp and a low release over the opening. It preserves the existing tool orientation unless the measured opening-axis alignment requires a yaw change. A small lift tests retention by comparing independently observed package motion with actual hand motion. Subsequent motion uses bounded Jev-driven feedback leases and phase-local visual obstruction reviews, not an unavailable swept-volume certificate. Tracking is optional: semantically persistent physical landmarks provide executable registration recovery. Checkpoints preserve reference provenance, acknowledged jaw transitions, retention evidence, and release intent across repairs. After opening, the method withdraws, measures the released package in a fresh view, and checks both its actual relation to the bowl and stability. Unsupported geometry, ambiguous correspondence, observed obstruction, stalled progress, or exhausted evidence budgets produce an incomplete result with numerical diagnostics.

## Model revision1 rationale (historical)

The failure was an invalid grasp precondition, not an observed collision: the planned pad center was 0.000387 m above the package top, but its finite contact band still overlapped the side by about 0.00807 m. Replace the positive-inset requirement with the exact translated-interval overlap constraints intersected with measured finger-to-table clearance. A pad center need not lie inside the package's side-height interval. Preserve the existing acquisition checkpoint, tracker recovery, bounded Jev control, retention test, transfer, and release verification. Add a current-state geometric check immediately before closure, including after any registration-recovery motion; this checks usable side overlap and support clearance without treating them as contact or retention evidence. No motion has occurred in the recorded episode, and the unchanged acquisition logic can reuse its same-frame measurements.

## Model revision2 rationale (historical)

The recorded failure rejected a valid-looking planar registration solely because its largest spread was below a fixed 0.010 m threshold. At frame 135 the tracker had 120/120 inliers, RMS 0.00005425 m, two useful spreads of 0.009705 and 0.005236 m, and uncertainty floor 0.001658 m. Replace that absolute-size gate with two resolved in-plane directions relative to the reported uncertainty, while retaining reference continuity, inlier, consensus, and residual checks. Log the actual acceptance thresholds before recovery can fail. Reuse the saved tracker and geometry with a fresh update; do not reset the physical episode. The coupled approach repair recognizes an already aligned, open, nearby hand instead of needlessly replaying the high approach. If registration recovery changes the hand pose during entry, permit one re-entry and then recheck current geometry. Finally, observe the package after closure without interpreting closure or registration alone as retention. The existing measured lift, transfer checks, low-release assessment, and post-release stability verification remain required.

## Model revision3 rationale (historical)

The release gate failed because the requested centering correction was smaller than the motion routine's 0.003 m early-return tolerance. At frame 234, the measured radial extent plus current uncertainty was 0.04871535 m, exceeding the conservative usable radius of 0.0480066 m, while the package center was only about 0.00175 m from the bowl center. The second correction therefore produced no motion and repeated the same failed measurement. Preserve the release clearance criterion, saved geometry, tracker reference, and measured retention. Derive a tighter local pose tolerance from the radial margin available after recentering, execute a bounded reviewed correction, and reassess the actual object geometry after each lease rather than requiring exact pose convergence. Permit at most three corrections, log the numerical margins, and stop explicitly if the controller deadband prevents further evidence-producing progress. Release and post-release relation/stability checks remain required.
