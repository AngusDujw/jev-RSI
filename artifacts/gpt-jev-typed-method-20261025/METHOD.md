## Model-authored revision rationale

The recorded failure was a route-budget mismatch, not an observed obstacle: every self-occluded review reduced progress to 0.035 m, so five reviews exhausted the route loop while the robot remained safely above the carton. This repair preserves the executed approach and historical measured invariants. It obtains one explicit authorization for each bounded local phase, separates self-occlusion from external obstruction, and executes that phase in at most six 0.08 m pose-feedback segments. It does not require visibility of the later transfer corridor or direct visibility of both vertical contact faces before an open-jaw staging descent. Measured grasp width, conservative aperture estimate, table clearance, and pad overlap are supplied explicitly to the reviewer. Intermediate noncontact registrations provide new object observations and tracker appearance updates during descent; unexpected carton displacement stops the approach. Existing retention testing, release verification, bounded observation recovery, checkpoints, and Jev direction questions remain intact.
