## Design

- Five tasks, four paired official layouts (0–3), four conditions: pure GPT, Jev-gated reuse, fixed mean interval, fixed maximum interval. Planned total: 80 attempts.
- Paper Jev-subsection regime: zero-shot B25; GPT-6 Astra/xhigh predicts 20 actions. Pure execution cap: five actions; reconstructed Jev gate can approve one additional five-action segment.
- This extends the Jev subsection to five tasks; it does not reproduce the main table's one-shot J12/B12 Memory-task regime. Historical task-selection scores are not comparable matched-setting baseline scores.
- GPT transport: local ChatGPT Pro through an SSH tunnel; no OpenAI Platform planner calls.
- Server project: `/home/ykj/project/jev-rsi-roboicl`. Initially eight simulator workers on four RTX 4090 GPUs, two workers per card. No saved experiment images or videos.
- Mean and maximum controls will be fitted per task from observed Jev intervals after all four attempts terminate. Unscored/policy-error attempts keep their closed intervals; their final right-censored tail is excluded. No failed attempt is silently replaced.

The supplemental root contains a byte-identical copy of the frozen interval file (SHA256 8980812467219de8bd7ea3ded9b671465e12b38a996e80215b502bea9ad89138), not a refit.

Jev uses an independent fixed-endpoint relay on 7913, the same official model and unchanged image-free request. Network/408/429/5xx retry at most three times, returning the first valid response. Backoffs are 15 and 45 seconds. The configured upstream socket timeout is 60 seconds and client socket timeout 300 seconds; urllib timeouts are not absolute whole-request wall-clock deadlines. Every upstream attempt is audited, and unknown failed-request usage remains unknown. No action or prompt adaptation is made from failed rollouts.

## Limits and artifacts
