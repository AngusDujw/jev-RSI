# RoboICL action reuse — final results, 2026-10-21

## Summary

This evaluates a disclosed reconstruction of the paper's Jev-subsection gate: GPT predicts twenty actions, executes five, and image-free Jev may approve one additional five-action segment. Five tasks use layouts 0–3, one rollout per condition/layout. GPT is gpt-6-astra/xhigh through the workstation ChatGPT Pro account; Jev is the real official jev-1.13.0 API. No Platform planner credentials were used.

The original 80 attempts are retained. Infrastructure-only supplements were registered separately (35 + nine + one). **All 125 physical attempts have terminated: 79 native scores and 46 unscored outcomes.** The pure press-layout-1 supplement was stopped after an observed IK-rejection/safety-hold loop; it has no native score and is excluded from native comparisons.

Main observation: Jev reduces completed GPT planning responses, but its observed success advantage over either fixed schedule is only one of twenty layouts. Runtime does not improve over the fixed schedules in this batch. The comparisons are exploratory, with four layouts per task and one stochastic rollout per condition/layout.

## Paired successful layouts: equal-outcome cost check

These outcome-selected subsets do not estimate whole-benchmark performance. They help show whether savings persist when both policies succeed.

| Comparator | Layouts where both succeed | Comparator GPT completions | Jev GPT completions | Reduction | Jev additional votes | Comparator / Jev action steps | Comparator / Jev wall minutes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Pure GPT (5) | 10 | 404 | 256 | 36.63% | 244 | 1980 / 2262 | 189.58 / 237.70 |
| Fixed mean (9/9/8/9/9) | 13 | 384 | 339 | 11.72% | 328 | 3232 / 2968 | 249.93 / 319.93 |
| Fixed maximum (10) | 12 | 348 | 333 | 4.31% | 318 | 3324 / 2833 | 217.20 / 277.23 |

Against fixed maximum on the twelve mutually successful layouts, Jev saves only fifteen GPT completions (4.31%) while adding 318 Jev votes. On all twenty layouts, the success-discordant pairs are Jev-only / comparator-only = 2/1 versus fixed mean and 3/2 versus fixed maximum. A one-layout net success difference is weak evidence for a general accuracy improvement.

An accounting identity helps interpret call reductions: completed GPT responses = environment action steps / mean steps between completed GPT responses. Different trajectory lengths and failure termination points affect this statistic. Fewer planning calls alone does not identify a superior gate, shorter successful motion, or faster execution.

## Pure press layout 1: censored IK-stall diagnostic

The original attempt stopped unscored after a PhysX/invalid-joint-state failure. The additional diagnostic ran 6758.64 seconds and was stopped by the operator after persistent left-arm IK failures.

At the recorded stop: 240 GPT requests, 231 zero-execution receipts, only 36 executed GPT-command actions, and 77 executed local observed-state safety holds. The native environment counter reached 113, but its growth after the 36th GPT-command action came from the released local-safety-hold mechanism. Hold issue/result records are distinct; count executed result records, not all 154 hold-log entries.

The stopping decision was **post hoc**, made to terminate an observed rejection loop. Return code is 130; there is no native score. Do not label this a native zero, impute a success/failure, or use its censored runtime as an equal-quality cost saving. All its requests, completed responses and unconsumed outputs remain in the raw ledger. The cause of persistent IK infeasibility is not resolved by this batch. No controller rescue, prompt change, guard weakening or driver change was applied.

The headline action-step metric uses the official environment action counter, including released safety holds. Native-scored episodes were audited: committed GPT-command steps + executed local holds exactly equal this counter. Interrupted Jev episodes can have an executed prefix whose tool receipt was never committed; those censored prefixes remain visible in observation logs.

## Protocol and provenance limits

- Official RoboICL source: 07d6e1c5387710e2de9240724b0015bbf0c95c6e, [Mosi-AI/RoboICL](https://github.com/Mosi-AI/RoboICL).
- Follow the [paper Jev subsection](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34261v1): zero-shot B25, twenty predictions, five-action prefix and one optional five-action suffix. This extends its two-task/five-layout panel to five tasks/four layouts; it is not the main table's one-shot J12/B12 Memory regime.
- The author did not release the gate prompt. This is an explicit reconstruction using plan note, robot proprioception, issued commands and tracking receipts, with no RGB or privileged object state sent to Jev.
- The GPT execution-bound wording is five in pure, ten in Jev, and the configured cap in fixed conditions. Fixed maximum and Jev share upper bound ten. This tests complete policy conditions; it does not isolate gate reasoning from every interaction-history or scheduling difference.
- Pure source: 920d06f3; original Jev: ef9808c7; original fixed and first/second supplements: 4590db33. All 124 earlier case allocations were verified before deploying bc12130e, whose changes affect Pro retry transport and the optional new loopback endpoint. Stage 3 records bc12130e.
- Pro route is the workstation signed-in ChatGPT account, model gpt-6-astra/xhigh; no Platform planner key. Jev route uses the separately authorized credential and pins jev-1.13.0. Unknown failed-request usage is retained.
- Server company-server-2-ykj, project /home/ykj/project/jev-rsi-roboicl; RTX 4090 GPUs 0–3, at most two simulators per card. No GPU/CUDA driver or shared Python/Conda permission changes.
- All incoming model-visible text is English. Outer experiment-management decisions are not injected into either model.

All committed GPT receipts request twenty predictions and respect the condition's execution cap. Native step accounting, frozen-file hashes and media audits pass: zero saved trial media and zero embedded-image JSONL files.
