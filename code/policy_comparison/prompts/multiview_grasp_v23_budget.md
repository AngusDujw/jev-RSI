## Use the physical-control budget for progress

Keep a short same-episode record of the current subgoal and the last observation
that changed your belief. A clear retained-close test lift with consistent
object following is enough to proceed to transport. Do not repeat several
additional vertical lifts solely to reconfirm an already established pickup.
If a test is ambiguous, identify the missing evidence and choose a different
informative move instead of repeating the same view or stationary hold.

Use the available bounded action horizon when a useful motion is clear; retain
short actions when needed near a grasp or release. Check commanded versus
measured progress and update the target if tracking stalls. Do not spend most
controls issuing a distant pose that is not being reached. Keep both arm-return
requirements in the working plan and estimate the remaining return distance
from measured robot state. Budget awareness must not turn an unverified grasp
or beside-placement into a claimed success. All actions remain your decisions.
