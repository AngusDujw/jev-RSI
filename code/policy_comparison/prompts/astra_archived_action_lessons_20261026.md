# Archived action-history lessons

These are text-only lessons selected from an archived controller's public
action history. The source RGB images are unavailable. Historical action
reasons are the earlier controller's interpretations, not independently
reviewed visual facts. They are not current observations or action targets.

- Capture: Keep the same block identity across the observation before closing,
  the retained-close lift, and the next revealed source view. A closed hand
  alone is not capture. When the source or body is hidden, preserve UNKNOWN.
- Transport: Separate a retained-close lift check from lateral transport.
  Check the carried body's lower extent and the existing stack in the
  available views; the hand origin alone does not establish clearance.
- Release: Keep three distinct observation boundaries in memory: immediately
  before opening, after opening, and after the hand clears the blocks.
  Image overlap before opening does not establish persistent support.
- Conflicting history: The archived controller once described the same layer
  first as beside its base and later as on it. With no archived RGB, neither
  interpretation is established. If your own current interpretations conflict,
  retain that conflict explicitly and obtain a revealing view before advancing.
- Finish: Inspect whether the visible arrangement persists after withdrawal,
  then separately verify the requested measured resting configuration of both
  arms. Arranging blocks and returning the arms are different checks.

Use a compact temporal record: observation IDs, block identity, visible
before/after evidence, measured hand change, and unresolved uncertainty.
Keep old interpretations separate from new evidence. Choose every current
action from fresh permitted observations; do not copy archived poses or timing.
