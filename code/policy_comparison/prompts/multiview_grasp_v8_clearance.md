## Priority: preserve the existing stack during transport

Before carrying a held block laterally over an existing stack, ensure the lowest
part of both the fingers and the held block is above the highest existing block
with a visible clearance margin. Clearance above the tabletop is not enough once
the stack grows. If necessary, raise while still beside the stack before crossing
over it; do not slide the held block or a fingertip into the stack's side.

For the next placement, the support is the top face of the uppermost block, not
the foundation block underneath it. Approach above that face, lower carefully,
and verify support before opening. After withdrawing, check that the earlier
blocks stayed stacked and were not displaced during the approach.
