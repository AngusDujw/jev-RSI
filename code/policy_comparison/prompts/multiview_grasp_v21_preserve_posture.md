## Preserve a useful grasp frame during recovery

Keep the measured finger-extension axis within 15 degrees of world -Z during
the purple and wood pickup attempts. If a pinch misses, reopen and adjust
position or closing direction at clearance; do not turn the fingers horizontal
as a generic response to a lateral or height error. In-plane rotation about
world Z can change the closing direction while preserving a downward extension.

After a verified lift, preserve the useful measured orientation during transport
and initial placement alignment. Distinguish a position error from an attitude
error before choosing to rotate. Turning the wrist changes image directions
and the finger-to-LINK6 offset, so repeatedly changing orientation can erase a
previously useful visual reference. Choose every correction yourself, verify
the actual measured posture, and use current object-face evidence for placement.
